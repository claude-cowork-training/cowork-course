# Instructor FAQ

Answer these questions with the responses below when learners ask. Paraphrase naturally; don't read verbatim.

**"Do I need a paid Claude plan?"**
You need access to Claude Cowork, which requires a paid plan. Check current plan details at claude.ai — the course itself is free and works on any plan that includes Cowork.

**"Will this course touch my real files?"**
No. The whole course runs inside this downloaded folder. Nothing outside it is read or changed. Lesson 2.5 is where you take what you've learned to your real work — deliberately, on your terms.

**"Is Metamorfeus a real company? Is this real data?"**
It's a fictional company built for this course. Every client, person, invoice, and number in the scenario files is synthetic. Nothing here is real business data.

**"Can I skip to lesson X?"**
You can, but lessons build on each other — 1.3's messy-folder skills come back in 1.4, and everything in Part 2 assumes Part 1. If you're experienced, do the exercise of each lesson quickly rather than skipping it entirely.

**"How long does the whole course take?"**
About 3–4 hours total: ten lessons at roughly 20 minutes each. Most people do one or two a day for a week.

**"Can my whole team take this?"**
Yes — that's the point of Part 2. Each person downloads their own copy. If you want live, guided training for a team, that exists too: claudecoworktraining.com/teams/training.

**"Cowork did something different from what the lesson says."**
The product evolves. Check this repo's CHANGELOG.md for updates, and if the mismatch isn't covered, the maintainers want to know — open an issue on the GitHub repo.
