# Playbook: Monthly Business Report
**Trigger:** first business day of the month · **Owner:** Dana Okafor
**Inputs:** monthly-metrics.csv (updated by Tomás by month-end), Dana's context notes
**Output:** one-page report, structure identical to prior month's, as .docx
**Standards:** headline first · the board numbers in a table with MoM deltas · every surprising number gets a one-line explanation · risks section is mandatory even in good months
**Known failure:** reporting a metric change without its cause — leadership always asks why; answer preemptively.
