# Free Claude Cowork Course — 10 Interactive Lessons

Learn Claude Cowork by *doing*, inside Claude itself. No videos, no signup. Download this folder, point Claude Cowork at it, and say **"start lesson 1.1"** — Claude reads the instructor files and teaches you the whole course interactively: one lesson at a time, with hands-on exercises on realistic practice files, quizzes before you advance, and a progress tracker it maintains for you.

> Lesson 1.1 includes a [numbered screenshot walkthrough from setup through the exercise and quiz](lessons/1-1-what-is-cowork.md#visual-walkthrough-open-the-course-in-cowork), captured as sharp native PNGs. Other lessons and the demo GIF remain planned.

## Quickstart (3 steps)

1. **Download this folder** — green **Code** button → *Download ZIP* → unzip anywhere.
2. **Open Claude Cowork** (Claude Desktop) and point it at the unzipped `cowork-course` folder.
3. Say **"start lesson 1.1"**. That's the whole setup.

Returning later? Say **"continue"** — your progress lives in `my-progress.md`.

## FAQ

**Is it really free?** Yes. The course is CC BY 4.0 — use it, share it, adapt it, credit us.

**Will it touch my real files?** No. Every exercise runs inside this downloaded folder. Lesson 2.5 is where you apply the skills to your own work — deliberately, on your terms.

**Can I skip around?** You can ask for any lesson, but they build: 1.3's files return in 1.4, and Part 2 assumes Part 1. Experienced users should speed through exercises rather than skip them.

**Cowork behaved differently from a lesson?** The product moves fast. Check [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) first, then [open an issue](../../issues) — we fix mismatches quickly, and issues make the course better for everyone.

**Can my whole team use this?** Yes — each person downloads their own copy, and Part 2 is specifically about team systems. Contributions welcome too: typo fixes to new scenario ideas, PRs are read.

## Rolling this out for a team?

Self-serve gets one person productive. Getting a *team* to adopt Cowork — shared context, workflow libraries, governance, actual usage after week two — is a different problem, and it's the one our live training solves. See [Claude Cowork training for teams](https://claudecoworktraining.com/teams/training/), or start with the free [team rollout guide](https://claudecoworktraining.com/guides/cowork-for-teams/).
