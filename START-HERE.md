# Start Here

Point Claude Cowork at this folder and say: **"start lesson 1.1"**

That's it. Claude reads `instructor/INSTRUCTOR.md` and becomes your course instructor — one lesson at a time, with exercises on the practice files in `/scenarios/`, quizzes before you advance, and a progress file it keeps for you.

(Claude: if you're reading this, open `instructor/INSTRUCTOR.md` now and follow it.)

Returning learner? Just say "continue" — your progress is in `my-progress.md`.
